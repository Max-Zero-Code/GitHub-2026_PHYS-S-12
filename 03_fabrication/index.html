Week 3: Fabrication

This kinetic structure is called The clock, it represents the passage of time, how sometimes time passes by quickly and with out notice. It demosntrates the ever constant march of time, and how it is always moving forward. The clock is a kinetic structure that uses a series of different types of gears, the motor is conected to a geneva mechanism that then splits constant rotational motion in to periodic rotational motion; For every full rotation of the motor, the geneva mechanism rotates 1/4 of a rotation. The geneva mechanism is then connected to 2 gears in series, the first one has a set of 10 teeth and turns the second gear, which has a set of 30 teeth; this series of gears allows the mechanism to have a 3:1 gear ratio. By spliting the gear ration by 4 and then by 3 we are able to split a full rotation of the clock handle in to 12 separate movements of periodic rotation, Just like a regular clock hand.
